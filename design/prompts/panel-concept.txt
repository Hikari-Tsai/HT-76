Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: High fidelity design board for a JUCE 1176-inspired FET compressor plugin, showing two switchable views of the SAME plugin.
Primary request: Design an exceptionally polished, realistic audio plugin UI with one traditional rack faceplate and one contemporary graphical dynamics view, inspired by the clarity and real-time level visualization of FabFilter. Original product name "1176 / FIELD EFFECT". No FabFilter or Universal Audio logos.
Composition: A landscape 2048 by 1536 design board, very dark background. Two distinct full-width front-facing plugin windows stacked vertically, enough space for all controls and labels. The upper traditional rack window occupies about 38 percent of the board and the lower modern graphical window about 52 percent. Small tasteful section labels CLASSIC RACK and DYNAMIC VIEW. Absolutely straight-on orthographic UI, no perspective, no computer or studio room, no hands.
Classic rack: brushed charcoal anodized metal with understated texture, dark rack ears and realistic flush screws, slim utility header with 1176 at left and segmented RACK / DYNAMIC at right. Large beautifully rendered machined black knobs with ivory pointers for INPUT and OUTPUT on left, smaller ATTACK and RELEASE knobs in center with slow-to-fast scales, vertical mechanical ratio buttons labeled 4, 8, 12, 20 plus a separate amber ALL button. Cream backlit analog VU meter on the right, delicate black needle, proper VU arc. Compact GR / IN / OUT meter selection. A slim bottom strip of cyan input and warm-white output stereo meters. Warm white legends and restrained amber active lights. Elegant historically informed physical proportions. The traditional rack must feel convincingly tactile, not a flat web dashboard.
Modern view: same brand and RACK / DYNAMIC segmented switch, deep graphite/navy canvas. A very large scrolling level-history plot dominates the upper two thirds: teal input amplitude envelope behind a finer warm ivory compressed output envelope, and a separate amber gain-reduction trace descending from a 0 dB reference. Horizontal time axis and precise dBFS grid, three tiny legends INPUT / OUTPUT / REDUCTION. Keep chart believable and readable, with signal pulses and varied transients; do not use a frequency spectrum. On the right, three tall precise meter strips labeled IN / GR / OUT with readable dB scale and peak readouts, stereo pair for input and output and one amber GR bar. Below the graph, a clean continuous control deck with rotary INPUT, ATTACK, RELEASE, OUTPUT controls and segmented RATIO 4 / 8 / 12 / 20 / ALL; the values 12.0 dB, 200 us, 400 ms, -6.0 dB shown clearly. Discreet bypass button. No threshold knob, no multiband controls, no EQ nodes, no fake sidebars, no big marketing slogans. Enough negative space to read at normal plugin size.
Visual style: high-end professional music software, precise typography, meticulously aligned controls, subtle depth and small bevels, near-black graphite with teal #66d7c6 and amber #f1b765 accents, typography similar to Inter and compact monospaced numbers. Minimal restrained glow, strong small-text contrast, crisp edges. Exact same visual identity in both views. Focus on usable plugin panel design rather than promotional artwork.
